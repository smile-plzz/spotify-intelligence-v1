Data & permissions

Read-only access. We never post, modify playlists, or control playback on your behalf.

user-read-recently-played Your recent listening history, to build sessions and time-of-day patterns.
user-top-read Your top artists and tracks across short, medium and long time ranges.
user-library-read Saved tracks, albums and playlists in your library.
user-read-currently-playing What's playing right now, for the Live view.
user-read-playback-state Playback progress and device state for the currently-playing card.

Disconnect anytime from Settings — your synced data can be cleared with it.
